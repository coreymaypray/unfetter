About Us

The Real Cut Above was established in 1992 by Mr. Keith Potter and has proudly served the Indianapolis area for over 30 years, delivering top-tier haircare services in a welcoming space.

As a barbershop and salon, we're dedicated to offering exceptional grooming and styling for men, women, and families alike, blending timeless techniques with modern flair.

More than just a place to get a haircut, The Real Cut Above has been a cornerstone of the community, mentoring young barbers, teaching life skills, and supporting small businesses.
